Welcome to Echo Loom. This walkthrough uses sample AI responses and prepared portrait assets.

Start with a few words about someone you would like to meet.

Make a small change. The same character identity is preserved.

Generate a portrait from the appearance profile, then preview the result before selecting it.

Edit the selected portrait with a simple instruction. The original image stays available.

Compare saved versions and switch back at any time, without recreating the character.

Chat with the saved character. Their latest profile guides the conversation.

Ready for a shared scene? The activity button is always visible.

Describe the scene, your relationship, and anything you want to avoid.

Your invitation becomes a scene card in this conversation.

Story mode presents narration, character dialogue, and AI-assisted player lines.
